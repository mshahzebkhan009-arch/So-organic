Got Questions?

Everything you need to know.

Who is the best candidate for So Organic Professional Treatments?

Ideal for clients with frizzy, damaged, or chemically processed hair across fine, coarse, curly, permed, or relaxed textures.

What kind of finish can I expect?

Frizz-free, radiant shine, silky manageability with natural body and bounce retained depending on your original hair texture.

Is it safe for color-treated or highlighted hair?

Yes, our treatments condition and seal the hair cuticle, helping enhance color vibrancy and shielding hair from daily damage.

How long do the professional treatments last?

Results last between 10 to 12 weeks when paired consistently with the So Organic home aftercare maintenance range.
